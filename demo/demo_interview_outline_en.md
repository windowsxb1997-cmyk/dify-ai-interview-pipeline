# Interview Outline (Demo) — Over-ear Headphones at the Gym

> Synthesized for the AI-interviewer demo. English, North American gym-goers, ~15 minutes.

## 1. Warm-up & Background
- Tell me a bit about your workout routine. How often do you go to the gym, and what kind of training do you usually do?
- What kind of gym is it — commercial gym, campus gym, office gym, or apartment gym?

## 2. Current Headphone Usage
- Do you currently wear headphones when you work out? Which model?
- Is it over-ear, in-ear, or something else? Why that choice?
- How often do you wear over-ear headphones specifically at the gym — every session, sometimes, rarely?

## 3. Core Motivation
- What made you start wearing over-ear headphones at the gym in the first place?
- What do you get out of it — focus, immersion, blocking noise, style, sound quality?

## 4. Noise Cancelling / Isolation
- How important is noise cancelling for you at the gym? Do you actually turn ANC on?
- Can you describe the noise environment at your gym? What bothers you most?

## 5. Heat / Sweat / Cleaning
- Let's talk about heat and sweat. How big of an issue is it for you?
- What happens to the ear pads? Do you clean them? How?
- Has heat or sweat ever made you skip wearing them or cut a session short?

## 6. Stability / Fit
- Which exercises make the headphones shift or feel unstable? (bench press, running, jumping, burpees…)
- Have you ever stopped doing a movement because of the headphones?

## 7. Device Combo & Carry
- Do you carry other earbuds as well? When do you switch between them?
- How do you carry the headphones to the gym — in the bag, around the neck?

## 8. Priorities
- If you had to pick the three things you care about most for gym headphones — noise cancelling, sound quality, stability, weight, clamping comfort, heat control, sweat resistance, easy cleaning, battery, looks, price, brand — what would they be, and in what order?

## 9. Alternatives & Repurchase Triggers
- Have you considered other options — like just using AirPods or bone conduction? What's stopping you or attracting you?
- What would make you buy a new pair of over-ear headphones specifically for the gym? What would it need to have?
- Roughly what price range would you consider reasonable?

## 10. Closing
- Is there anything about wearing over-ear headphones at the gym that I didn't ask about but you think is important?
