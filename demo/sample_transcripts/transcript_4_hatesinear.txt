Mock Interview Transcript — Persona 4: Hates In-Ear Type
Interviewee: Sofia R. (ID: US-AUS-014), 29, nurse, Austin
I = Interviewer, R = Respondent

I: When did you last wear over-ear headphones at the gym?

R: This morning before my shift. I do strength training three times a week at a commercial gym near the hospital. I wear Bose QC45. Here's the thing: I physically cannot use in-ear buds. My ear canals are small and sensitive — every earbud I've tried hurts within twenty minutes, and they constantly fall out. Over-ear is literally my only option, in the gym and everywhere else.

I: So how does the QC45 perform for training?

R: It's the lightest over-ear I could find, that's why I bought it. Comfort is my whole decision. The clamp is gentle, ears don't get crushed, I can wear it my whole 75-minute session. Sound and ANC are just okay, and I accept that trade-off happily.

I: Any pain points?

R: The pads get sweaty and there's no way to deep-clean them, which grosses me out — I wipe them with sanitizing wipes, hospital habit. They also slip a bit when I do hip thrusts against the bench. And I worry about long-term durability with daily sweat. If the pads were replaceable and washable, that would be amazing.

I: Rank your priorities.

R: Comfort and light weight, tied for first. Then cleanability — washable pads would be huge for me. Then stability. ANC and sound are at the bottom; I just need podcasts and lo-fi beats.

I: Would you buy a gym-specific over-ear?

R: If it were light, washable, and under $300 — immediately. Nobody designs for people like me. Bone conduction doesn't work for me either, the vibration feels strange. Over-ear or nothing.

I: Friends who wear over-ears at the gym?

R: My coworker does, same reason — earbuds hurt her too. It's more common than brands think.

[End]
