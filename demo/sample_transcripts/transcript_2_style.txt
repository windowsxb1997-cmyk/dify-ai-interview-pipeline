Mock Interview Transcript — Persona 2: Style Display Type
Interviewee: Chloe D. (ID: US-NYC-012), 24, fashion marketing associate, New York
I = Interviewer, R = Respondent

I: When did you last wear over-ear headphones at the gym?

R: Yesterday evening. I go to an Equinox in Manhattan four times a week. I wear my AirPods Max — the purple one. Honestly? Half the reason I wear it is the look. The gym is a social space; everyone sees everyone. A big headphone says "I'm in my zone, don't talk to me," but it also has to look good doing it.

I: Tell me about your training and how the headphone fits in.

R: I do strength circuits and Pilates-style work, about an hour. Nothing super high-impact. The AirPods Max matches my outfit — I literally plan gym fits around it sometimes. Sounds vain, but the aesthetic is part of the motivation to go. Beats used to be the culture pick; I had a red Beats Studio in college because of the whole music-and-NBA thing. Now the Max is the status object.

I: Any functional complaints?

R: It's heavy — after an hour my neck notices. And I worry about sweat ruining it, it's expensive! I bought silicone covers for the ear cups because makeup and sweat stain the fabric. Foundation on the mesh is a real problem. Battery is fine. ANC is good — the gym playlists are awful, so I block them out and play my own.

I: Rank your priorities.

R: Style first, obviously. Then ANC. Then... comfort, I guess, because of the weight. Sound quality — fine, I'm not an audiophile. Brand matters too; it IS the style.

I: Would you buy a gym-specific over-ear from a non-fashion brand?

R: It would have to look incredible. I'd need to see it on Instagram first, on people whose style I trust. Sweat-proof fabric in cute colors, that's the dream. Function alone doesn't sell me.

I: Friends who wear over-ears at the gym?

R: Several girls at my Equinox do. We definitely notice each other's colors. It's a vibe.

[End]
