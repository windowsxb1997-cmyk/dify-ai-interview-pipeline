Mock Interview Transcript — Persona 3: Audio Quality First Type
Interviewee: Marcus T. (ID: US-BOS-013), 33, architect, Boston
I = Interviewer, R = Respondent

I: When did you last wear over-ear headphones at the gym?

R: Last night. I train at my apartment building's gym, three to four times a week, mostly strength machines and dumbbells, quiet hours around 10 PM. I wear Sennheiser Momentum 4. I chose it purely for sound — I compared it against Sony and Bose for weeks, read every review, listened in-store twice.

I: What does sound quality do for your training?

R: Everything. I listen to jazz and classical while lifting — Coltrane, sometimes Bach. People think that's weird, but high-resolution, detailed sound puts me in a focused, almost meditative state. A wide soundstage makes a small gym feel like a private studio. If the headphones distort at high volume or the bass is muddy, my whole session feels off.

I: How do you feel about noise cancelling?

R: Important but secondary. My gym is quiet at night anyway. I'd take superior drivers over superior ANC any day. Actually heavy ANC pressure bothers my ears during long sessions.

I: Any complaints?

R: The Momentum 4 gets warm after an hour. And touch controls misfire when I wipe sweat — pauses the music mid-set, infuriating. Physical buttons would be better for the gym. Pads absorb sweat; I wipe them after every session.

I: Rank your priorities.

R: Sound quality, then sound quality, then sound quality. Fine — comfort second, battery third. Style doesn't matter to me. Price doesn't either, within reason; I paid $380 happily.

I: Would a gym-specific headphone interest you?

R: Only if it sounds reference-grade. Sweat-proof means nothing if the drivers are compromised. But honestly, a waterproof Momentum with physical buttons would be an instant buy.

I: Friends who wear over-ears at the gym?

R: None. People think I'm eccentric. Maybe I am.

[End]
