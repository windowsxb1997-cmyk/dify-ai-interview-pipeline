Mock Interview Transcript — Persona 6: Occasional Replacement Type
Interviewee: Tyler B. (ID: US-DEN-016), 23, grad student, Denver
I = Interviewer, R = Respondent

I: When did you last wear over-ear headphones at the gym?

R: Hmm, like two weeks ago? Maybe three. Okay so — I'm mainly an earbuds guy. AirPods Pro for running and most gym sessions. The over-ear, an old Beats Studio 3, shows up at the campus gym maybe once or twice a month, basically when my AirPods are dead or I forgot them at home. It's my backup plan, not a habit.

I: Walk me through one of those backup sessions.

R: Sure — last time, AirPods battery was at 5%, so I grabbed the Beats off my desk. Did chest and arms, forty minutes. It was... fine? The clamp is tight, which is actually good for movement, but my ears got sore by the end. Sound is mediocre. I wouldn't choose it on purpose, but it beat silence.

I: How do you feel about the differences vs your earbuds?

R: The Beats isolates more, which is nice when the gym is crowded. But it's hot, heavier, and I'm always aware of it. With AirPods I forget I'm wearing anything. For running I'd never use the Beats — bouncing city.

I: Rank your priorities for gym audio.

R: Convenience first — smallest, easiest. Then stability. Sweat-proof third. Honestly I've never thought about buying anything for the gym specifically.

I: Would you buy a gym-specific over-ear headphone?

R: Nah. My AirPods cover 90% of sessions. The backup situation happens rarely enough that spending money on it feels silly. If the Beats dies I probably wouldn't replace it.

I: Friends who wear over-ears at the gym?

R: One powerlifter guy at my gym does, every session, some big Sony. He swears by it. For me that level of commitment to a headphone is funny. To each their own.

[End]
