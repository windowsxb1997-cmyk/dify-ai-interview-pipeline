Mock Interview Transcript — Persona 5: Office-Commute Incidental Type
Interviewee: Kevin Z. (ID: US-CHI-015), 31, financial analyst, Chicago
I = Interviewer, R = Respondent

I: When did you last wear over-ear headphones at the gym?

R: Tuesday after work. Here's my situation: my Sony WH-1000XM4 lives in my work backpack. It's my commute and office headphone — ANC on the L train, focus at my desk. Our office building has a corporate gym on the third floor, and I lift there three times a week after work. The headphone is already with me, so I just keep it on. I never bought it for the gym; the gym just happens to be in its path.

I: How does it perform in that gym context?

R: Fine, honestly. The gym is small and not too loud. I do machines and dumbbells, 45 minutes. ANC covers the ventilation hum. I don't baby it, but I also wouldn't bring it if it meant carrying it specially — it's incidental. Convenience is the whole story.

I: Any annoyances?

R: It's bulky in my bag alongside the laptop. Sweat on the pads makes me mildly nervous about longevity since it's my daily driver — if it dies, my commute dies too. I wipe it quickly after workouts. Slipping happens on bench but I just adjust.

I: Rank your priorities.

R: ANC first — that's the commute and office talking. Battery second. Portability third — I wish it folded flatter. Gym-specific stuff like sweat resistance is nice-to-have, not a purchase driver.

I: Would you buy a headphone designed for the gym?

R: No. I wouldn't carry a second headphone, and I wouldn't compromise my commute headphone for gym features. One device, all contexts — that's me. Though if my next commute headphone happened to be sweat-proof, great.

I: Friends who wear over-ears at the gym?

R: A couple guys from the office gym, same setup — work headphone doing double duty.

[End]
